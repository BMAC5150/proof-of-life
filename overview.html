DOC. Overview
CLASS. Advance Review Copy

Four factions, one piece of software with the power to determine whose identity can continue to exist.

On a cold February morning in New York, a woman’s bank card is declined at a coffee counter. Then at an ATM. The same happens to sixty-seven thousand more New Yorkers. By nine the systems have quietly corrected themselves and the city forgets. Thirty blocks south, in a fintech start-up above a Chinatown dumpling shop, four engineers learn that the overnight glitch was theirs, and that the tool they built to tell banks who a person is can also tell every connected system that a person was never there at all.

Sovren cleans up duplicate customer records. One authoritative version of a person, trusted by thirty-one community banks across six states. Turned the other way, it can erase people from their own financial lives in a single night leaving no fingerprints behind. In a buried bunker outside Tehran, a cyber cell under pressure to prove it is still worth keeping as war approaches has already read the code. In a Manhattan tower, an analyst notices an anomaly in a funding deal she is closing and is manipulated into keeping quiet to keep her job. At Fort Meade, intel on a series of random cyber attacks nobody cares about grows on a commander’s desk.

Source of Truth is a geopolitical techno-thriller about competent people inside failing institutions, making rational decisions that add up to a catastrophe none of them can see coming. There are no explosions. There is a system that confirms who is real and a looming apocalypse that a group of disparate people must race to prevent.
