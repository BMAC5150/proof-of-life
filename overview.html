DOC. Overview
CLASS. Advance Review Copy

For four and a half hours one Tuesday morning in February, sixty-seven thousand people in New York City did not exist.

Their banks did not know them. They could not buy coffee or pay their rent. Their pharmacies did not know them.

The technical documentation from the software that did it had already been shared.

Aircraft carriers are closing on Iran’s coast. In a bunker outside Tehran, young Iranian operators are turning the software into the one weapon their country can aim at an American city. It is not a bomb. It is the power to erase the people who hold a city together.

The banks called it an outage. Washington was watching the wrong horizon. At Fort Meade, a Navy commander is the only one reading the right pile of paper.

Nobody in this book knows who to call.

For readers of Tom Clancy and John le Carré.
