Four records. Four people the system still recognizes.

Walker

NAMEDakota Walker
ROLEFounder, Sovren
LOC.Mott Street, NYC
FIRSTCH.01
STATUSIdentity Confirmed

Dakota Walker founded her fintech start-up Sovren out of a Chinatown walkup with three people she trusts and a dwindling bank balance she checks every morning. She wears a Tiffany key necklace to remind her that she holds the key to her own life.

In 2008, at eleven years old, her family was uprooted from their Upper East Side townhome to a New Jersey apartment when her father was laid off from Lehman Brothers.

Despite no network connections to trade on, she got a full academic scholarship to Princeton for Mathematics and Engineering. Recruiters mostly ignored her after graduating. She did basic contract tech and engineering work. Then COVID hit, and she got through it on barista shifts, serving tables and freelance coding found through the Jobbr app.

Overnight one Tuesday in February her company's software erased sixty-seven thousand people. By morning they were back. She told no one.

The Operator

NAMEKaveh Mohseni
ROLEIRGC Cyber Unit
LOC.Bunker outside Tehran
FIRSTCH.03
STATUSIdentity Confirmed

Kaveh Mohseni finished first in his class at Sharif. Sanctions closed every door except the one with the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps behind it. He walked through it.

He works in a bunker with a group of government-supported hackers thirty kilometers outside Tehran near Karaj, hacking into banks in other people’s countries to raise hard currency for the IRGC.

His unit has been graded adequate. In a country waiting to be bombed, adequate means expendable.

He raises cash for the regime by draining American bank accounts. He uses his skills to build something more terrible.

Anahita

NAMEAnahita Rostami
ROLESenior Analyst, Prescott Calder
LOC.902 Broadway, NYC
FIRSTCH.06
STATUSIdentity Confirmed

Anahita Rostami is twenty-seven, a senior analyst at the Manhattan technology venture capital firm Prescott Calder.

She is the daughter of Iranian exiles from Detroit. Both of her parents are gone, and the only family she has left is in Iran, and she has never met them. She graduated from the University of Michigan with degrees in computer science and business before being recruited to Prescott Calder.

Her boss tells her to sign the investor’s background check or clear out her desk. She receives a phantom text message that changes her life. She is now the most dangerous person in the room because she is the most alone.

Briggs

NAMEThomas “Creeper” Briggs
ROLECommander, U.S. Navy
LOC.Fort Meade, Maryland
FIRSTCH.05
STATUSIdentity Confirmed

Commander Thomas “Creeper” Briggs is fifty-eight and seven months into his last posting, a desk at Cyber Command in Fort Meade.

Home is a rented house that came furnished and otherwise empty except for a rescue dog. He has no family left and has given his entire adult life to the military.

His authority lives in restraint: he listens without interrupting, gives his people rules in a single sentence with nothing soft in it. He no longer trusts the superiors above him, who are ready to be briefed but not ready to listen, so he guards what he finds himself and quietly works around them until the institution is willing to act on it.
